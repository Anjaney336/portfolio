06 / Diligence before an actual investment

  • Reconcile the rent roll to executed leases, escalation clauses, expiries, deposits, rent-free periods and tenant credit.
  • Validate market rents, comparable sales, exit liquidity and cap-rate evidence for the exact micro-market.
  • Inspect title, permitted use, approvals, encumbrances and transaction structure with qualified advisers.
  • Obtain an engineering survey covering HVAC, lifts, fire systems, energy use, deferred maintenance and replacement capex.
  • Reconcile gross versus net rents, recoverable expenses, property taxes, insurance, financing terms and all transaction charges.

Limitations: annual timing, one rollover event, fixed occupancy outside rollover, no taxes, no GST, no lender fees, no refinancing, no inflation-linked market calibration and no probabilistic tenant defaults. The 6% acquisition-cost allowance is an illustrative aggregate, not a statement of applicable statutory charges. Reserve deductions represent actual retained cash unavailable to equity and are not recovered at exit. NOI DSCR excludes reserves and leasing capex; post-capex coverage is separately supplied in the CSV.
