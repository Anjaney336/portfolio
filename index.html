Bengaluru Office Investment & Asset Management

Acquisition decision → financing risk → operating review

Repository →

Question. What acquisition price compensates for lease-rollover and financing risk, and which operating variances require management attention?

  • Five-year illustrative underwriting: cash flows, capex, amortising debt, NPV, DSCR and price screening across 3 scenarios, 25 sensitivities and 2,000 stress draws.

Indian Real Estate Quant Research

The crossover: listed-property research and quantitative risk

Repository →

Question. Does a relative-value signal in listed Indian real estate survive out-of-sample testing, execution costs and downside-risk review?

  • 252/63-observation walk-forward testing, training-only cointegration screening with Holm correction, Kalman hedge ratios and lagged execution.
  • 1,675 historical OOS observations; negative baseline of −0.71% CAGR, −0.13 Sharpe and 10.71% maximum drawdown under hypothetical borrowing assumptions.
  • Rolling 252-day risk diagnostics: 1,423 forecasts and 71 exceedances. Explicitly reports 1,528 zero-return days; the near-5% exceedance rate is not proof of calibration.

Why it crosses roles. Investment teams can assess listed-property risk and reject unsupported theses; quant teams can inspect causal estimation, cost accounting, uncertainty and leakage tests.

Evidence. 27 tests, full historical ledger, forecast rows and hashes. Synthetic rate/HMM overlays remain separate from historical evidence; actual short availability is unverified.

Quant Execution Lab

Deterministic matching → transactional replay → execution research

Repository →

Question. Can execution schedules be compared fairly while preserving order priority, quantity accounting and reproducible event history?

  • Integer-tick FIFO matching, GTC/IOC orders, partial fills and cancellation; SQLite transactions, idempotent retries and corruption-detecting restart replay.
  • 20 tests, including 5,000 events checked against an independent reference and a separate 250-event persisted replay.
  • Immediate versus cumulative TWAP on 100 shared synthetic paths: fees, fill rates, missed-execution opportunity costs and 2,000 paired bootstrap resamples.

Finding. TWAP has 1.286 bp lower mean shortfall in the assumed generator. This is conditional synthetic evidence; replenishing liquidity can favour spreading orders. No production latency, empirical impact model or trading edge is claimed.
