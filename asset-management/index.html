Management priorities

The synthetic lease roll has half of current rent expiring within 365 days. Prioritise renewal discussions and compare retention terms against tenant-improvement and downtime costs. Monthly operating occupancy differs from the lease snapshot and is flagged for reconciliation, not silently overwritten.

Downside assumptions: cash rent −10%, operating expenses +10%, unchanged debt service. DSCR uses monthly NOI and excludes balloon payments. WALE is weighted by current monthly contractual rent and uses 365.25 days/year. No lease-up, tax, inflation or refinancing forecast is implied.

Trace the result

Actual NOI = budget NOI + occupancy effect + rate effect + collection effect + expense effect. Every asset and aggregate bridge reconciles to zero paise.
