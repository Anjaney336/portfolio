Bengaluru Investment & Asset Management

Acquisition underwriting → monthly leases → debt coverage → operating review

Decision and evidence

Consolidates the Bengaluru acquisition model, property operating review and financing stress. The monthly cohort model connects lease expiry and renewal assumptions to amortising debt and trailing coverage.

Finding. The synthetic 36-month lease case reaches 74% expected occupancy and 1.13× minimum trailing DSCR. The separate acquisition case still rejects the assumed INR100 crore entry price.

What entry price and financing can the property cash flows support?

  1. Underwrite the acquisition. Forecast rent, vacancy, operating expenses and capital expenditure before financing. Discount equity cash flows at the assumed required return; keep exit value and sale costs explicit.
  2. Size the loan. Debt capacity is the minimum of DSCR, LTV and debt-yield limits. The base case is DSCR-constrained at INR45.94 crore. At the assumed INR100 crore price, equity NPV is about −INR11.00 crore and minimum DSCR is 1.04x.
  3. Make the investment decision. The model supports a maximum price of about INR83.53 crore under its stated assumptions. Reject or reprice the INR100 crore case; validate market rents and physical capex before treating this as a real deal.
  4. Manage lease events. The separate monthly case links expiry, renewal probability, vacancy and leasing capex to amortising debt. A 1.13x minimum trailing DSCR is specific to that case, not the acquisition model.

Calculation: NOI = rental income − operating expenses; debt service = interest + principal; DSCR = NOI / debt service; equity cash = NOI − leasing capex − debt service.

Commercial real estate underwriting conventions

DCF discounting periods and terminal capitalization

Implemented: five-year hold, nominal INR, acquisition at t=0 and annual end-of-year cash flows at t=1–5. The assumed unlevered discount rate is 11%; the equity hurdle is 15%. This is periodic annual DCF, not mid-year discounting or XNPV.

Year-5 terminal value = year-6 NOI ÷ 8% exit cap rate. Deduct 2% selling costs and, for equity proceeds, the remaining year-5 debt. Year-6 NOI supports the exit valuation; it is not an extra operating cash receipt. All rates are assumptions.

PV = Σ[UCF_t / (1+r)^t] + net sale proceeds_5 / (1+r)^5

A terminal cap rate converts forward NOI into resale value; a discount rate values the multi-period cash-flow stream. Compare going-in and exit cap rates on consistent NOI and occupancy definitions.

Gross, Triple Net / NNN and modified-gross leases

Current model: aggregate landlord-paid operating expenses with no separate recovery schedule—a simplified gross-style cash-flow convention, not a verified lease structure. Gross leases generally include landlord-paid operating costs in rent; NNN leases allocate specified taxes, insurance and maintenance costs to tenants; modified-gross leases share expenses by contract.

For Bengaluru diligence, abstract base rent, CAM recovery, escalation, lock-in/break rights, deposits, rent-free periods and fit-out obligations. Compare INR/sqft/month rents on the same area and expense basis. CAM recovery, expense stops and deposits are not implemented in this model.

NOI, TI/LC and market net absorption

Income bridge: occupancy-adjusted rent − rollover downtime loss − landlord operating expenses = NOI. Deduct replacement reserves, tenant improvements (TI) and leasing commissions/fees (LC) before unlevered cash flow. Debt service is below NOI; distinguish NOI DSCR from post-capex coverage.

Net absorption = ending occupied area − beginning occupied area over a consistent market universe and period. It differs from gross leasing and must be read alongside new supply, vacancy and pre-leasing. These are market-diligence inputs: the case’s 90% occupancy and 5% rent growth remain assumptions, not calibrated Bengaluru forecasts.

Research views


Model mechanics

Monthly expected rent sums occupied cohort area × rent. Renewal and reletting branches carry separate capex timing. Debt interest uses opening balance × annual rate / 12; trailing DSCR is 12-month NOI divided by 12-month debt service. Acquisition valuation remains a separately parameterised teaching case.

From basic input reconciliation to stress analysis, each step has an auditable calculation trail in the repository.

Boundaries

Synthetic leases and underwriting assumptions; no actual rent roll, lender agreement or technical inspection. Expected renewal cash flow is not a calibrated probability forecast. Native Excel verification remains pending.

Offline research software, not a production trading or asset-management system.
